What we do

Majority backs early-stage deeptech startups in India working on livelihoods and climate. We're raising a $10 mn fund. Meanwhile we've made 4 angel investments and work hands-on with those founders. We also support a select few deeptech startups from our pipeline on product, go-to-market and fundraising, before we invest.

We also deliver projects for marquee customers such as BSE, IFAD and GAME. That revenue keeps us independent and funds new angel investments. It's the principle we train startups to live by: build a business customers pay for first, then raise investor money to grow it.

Startups we're looking for

Early-stage tech startups in India that raise the incomes of people living on a few dollars a day, or protect livelihoods from climate change.

Stage: you have your first customers and are working out how to grow them.

  • A real problem for a large customer base: farmers, small businesses, gig workers, women, workers whose jobs climate change puts at risk
  • Deeptech that makes the solution hard to copy
  • Customers who pay, or clear signs they will
  • Sound unit economics and a path to profit
  • Founders who talk to customers, learn fast and want hands-on support
  • India-first, with a path to Southeast Asia and Africa

Fintech and edtech fit when they serve livelihoods. Write to info@majority.fund with what you're building and who pays for it.
